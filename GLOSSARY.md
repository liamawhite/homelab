# Homelab

The homelab is the collection of home servers and services managed by this repository.

## Language

**Nix server**:
The NixOS-managed server that participates in the homelab alongside the Raspberry Pis.
_Avoid_: Desktop

**Active inventory**:
The homelab machines selected to participate in a deployment milestone. Machines excluded because of health problems remain outside this inventory until explicitly reintroduced.
